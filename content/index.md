## Hi, I'm Xiang Hao 👋

![[Assets/profile.jpg| 140]]

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, advised by [Prof. Jibin Wu](https://www.jibinwu.com/) and [Prof. Kay Chen Tan](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/dsai/people/academic-staff/tankaychen/).

My research lies at the intersection of **AI, audio signal processing, and hearing technologies**. I am particularly interested in speech enhancement, target speaker extraction, auditory attention decoding, neuromorphic audio, and real-time edge deployment for assistive listening.

I am currently visiting the [MRC Cognition and Brain Sciences Unit](https://www.mrc-cbu.cam.ac.uk/) at the University of Cambridge, working closely with Dr. François Guérit and Dr. Lidea Shahidi on machine-learning approaches for cochlear-implant coding strategies, within [Dr. Bob Carlyon's research group](https://www.mrc-cbu.cam.ac.uk/our-research/carlyon/).

I am always happy to connect with researchers working on related problems.
